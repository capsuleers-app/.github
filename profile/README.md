## Open-source projects

| Project | What it does |
|---|---|
| [**Capsuleers.Intel**](https://github.com/capsuleers-app/capsuleers.intel) | Desktop intel tool: paste your Local or a D-Scan and see who you're up against and what the hostile fleet is made of. |
| [**Capsuleers.IA**](https://github.com/capsuleers-app/capsuleers.ia) | Local, GPU-accelerated AI assistant for EVE Online, with cited answers in English or Italian. |
| [**Capsuleers.Settings**](https://github.com/capsuleers-app/capsuleers.settings) | Copy EVE client settings from one character to many. |
| [**eve-fit-engine**](https://github.com/capsuleers-app/capsuleers.fitting-engine) | Pyfa-parity fitting calculation engine for ships and Upwell structures, published on npm. |

Downloads are on each project's **Releases** page and on [capsuleers.app](https://capsuleers.app).
